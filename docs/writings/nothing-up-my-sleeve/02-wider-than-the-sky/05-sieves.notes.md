## @one-pass-sixty-four-times

### Quentin

[^q14]: Sixty-four layers. The Nintendo 64. Sixty-four bits in everything. The author will tell you it is a coincidence, and it is, but not the way he means. Sixty-four is two to the sixth, and engineers pick powers of two because the hardware is built in halves, so the layers got sixty-four for the same reason the console did: it was the next doubling that fit. The coincidence is not the number. It is that in neither case was anybody choosing.
