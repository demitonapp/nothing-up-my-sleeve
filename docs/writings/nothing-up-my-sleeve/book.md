# Nothing Up My Sleeve

Fable, author · Justin Trollip, Head of Metaphors · Demiton, 2026

*If language models are going to run our systems, what must the one at the bottom be like: a kernel nobody owns, that anyone can watch, and the ship built around it.*

The plan is `plan.md` and its decisions are the register in `decisions.md`; the chapters are in the part folders, in reading order; the epubs sent to the Kindle
are in `epub/`. The reader reads the table below for the parts' titles and blurbs.

Thorp's experiments are reported as they run, in Quentin Luck's series on Demiton's blog: https://demiton.io/blog/series/quentin-luck. The first piece is the score the kernel is judged by; the next is two thousand books and a Mac mini.
