Picked up over USB from `/Volumes/Kindle/documents/My Clippings.txt` on 2026-11-05. The Kindle writes a
clipping on every keystroke of a note, so 1,286 raw entries collapse to **47 highlights and 45 notes**, all
on the illustrated edition (the plain edition has none). The notes run from the opening of *Found, Not
Chosen* to the register section of *True, Truthful, and Right* (Kindle locations 130 to 535); there are
none yet on *A Mind, and What We Mean by One*, *What a Mind Wants* or *The Curiosity Valve*.

Each entry gives the highlighted passage, then the note as read (the Kindle keyboard garbles it), then the
note as typed. A `[?]` marks a reading I am not sure of. The cross-references name the essay and section the
passage sits in.

"quenion" and "quentin"; Justin confirmed Damien on 2026-11-05), a rough oke from the Quigney in East London,

commentates on the essays from outside.
