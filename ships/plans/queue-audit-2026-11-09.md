## What is missing and should be added

**F3 is the gap.** The causal test proved one thing that travels: a correlational map is mostly a map of reporters.
We have never run the head-causal read on **our own** best ship (the 9m, which holds all five cards), so we do not know
whether our ships have suppressors at all — heads that are actively costing them chaining. That is a `cmd:` line, a
few minutes on a small model, and it is the cheapest causal evidence the fleet can buy.
