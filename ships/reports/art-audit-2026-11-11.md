# The art audit, against the two-part style (11 November)

Justin: "can you audit all the images and the instructions and memory to make sure that we use the Risso
artstyle from 100 bullets but we now also have a time period and it's from the days of Drake."

## The instruction, as it now stands

`STYLE` in `thorp/dash/art/generate_art.py` carries both halves, and the module docstring records why:

- **Risso**: noir, heavy black ink, stark chiaroscuro, single light source, near-black ground, no text.
- **Drake**: Elizabethan England and the Spanish Main, 1577-1596 — oak, tarred rope, canvas, brass,
  lanterns, tallow, doublets and sea-cloaks; an anachronism is a defect. A ship of another nation's
  build is allowed only as a *prize* (Drake took the São Filipe), which is the period.

## The inventory

- **103 subjects** across 15 groups; **103 have an image** on disk.
- **20 are not in Drake's world** as written and must be re-prompted before they are regenerated.
- **83** are period-neutral at the subject level and are carried by the new `STYLE`.
- **Every image on disk predates the period**: they were generated with the old style, so all 103 are due a regeneration pass to pick up the Drake world. The console's
  plate was regenerated first, as the proof of the new style.

## What regeneration costs

`python -m thorp.dash.art.generate_art` (or `... <key>`) against fal, one call a subject: about four
minutes for the sixteen conflicts alone, and roughly ten for the whole inventory of 103. The images are
not versioned per style, so a regeneration overwrites in place; nothing else in the repository reads them.
